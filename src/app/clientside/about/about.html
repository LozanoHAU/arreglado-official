Our Community

History & Background

Barangay Pandacaqui is one of the barangays of the Municipality of Mexico in the Province of Pampanga, Central Luzon. With a rich agricultural heritage and a close-knit community, Pandacaqui has grown over the decades into a vibrant and progressive barangay.

The barangay takes pride in its hardworking residents, fertile lands, and community spirit. Through the collective efforts of its officials and constituents, Pandacaqui continues to strive for sustainable development, improved public services, and a safe and healthy environment for every household.

Mission

To deliver efficient, transparent, and responsive local governance that promotes the general welfare and development of all residents of Barangay Pandacaqui through inclusive programs, community participation, and sustainable services.

🌟

Vision

A progressive, peaceful, and self-sustaining barangay where every resident enjoys a safe environment, access to basic services, and equal opportunities for growth and prosperity under a God-centered and community-driven leadership.
