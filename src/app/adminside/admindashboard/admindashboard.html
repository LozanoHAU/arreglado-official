Quick Tips
💡
Start with the Hero Section Always fill in your event name, date, and a short description in the Hero Banner first — this is what residents see first.
📋
Add a Registration Form Use the Form section to collect attendee details. Customize the fields to fit your event — name, purok, mobile number, etc.
📅
Check the Calendar Often Use the Event Calendar to avoid scheduling conflicts and see all upcoming barangay activities at a glance.
🚀
Publish When Ready Hit "Publish Event" in the Event Builder to make your page live for residents to view. Unpublished pages are safe drafts.
